## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for institutional rigor, personal wealth stewardship, and analytical database interfaces. The visual language rejects ephemeral tech fads—dispensing with hyper-saturated purples, neon accents, floating glassy containers, and decorative gradients. Instead, it grounds the user experience in the visual dignity of classic financial institutions, central bank publications, and high-density terminal interfaces.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Financial Classicism** with **Modern Analytical Precision**:
- **Dignified & Authoritative:** High-contrast editorial serifs anchor key metrics and section headers, projecting durability, stability, and institutional trust.
- **Data-Dense & Pragmatic:** Information architecture prioritizes scannability, structural tabular presentation, ledger-style micro-grids, and monospaced numerical discipline.
- **Restrained & Tactile:** Flat planes with crisp slate borders, quiet micro-surfaces, deliberate hover states, and structural separation via borders rather than heavy atmospheric elevation.
- **Academic Rigor:** Metadata markers (such as categorization source, SQL-driven heuristic rules, execution status, and audit trails) are surfaced calmly as micro-badges with subdued tonality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of financial permanence through a deep naval baseline, disciplined monetary greens, balanced audit ambers, and warm architectural neutrals.

### Roles & System Palettes
- **Primary Anchor (`#0B1F3A` - Deep Navy):** Serves as the executive tone. Applied to persistent navigation, brand headers, primary callouts, and prominent data categorizations.
- **Primary Accent (`#1B9C6E` - Money Green):** Reserved for positive delta indications, net savings increments, successful state indicators, and actionable completion pathways.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D4A017` - Muted Warm Gold):** Applied for high-value account tiers, target milestones, analytical flags, and historical projections.
- **Flagged & Alert (`#D97706` / `#FEF3C7` - Amber Spectrum):** Used for threshold violations, budget over-utilization warnings, heuristic tagging notices, and manual reconciliation requirements.
- **Neutral Ink (`#1B2430` - Charcoal Ink):** Deep, legible graphite for optimal contrast across metrics and data rows. Subdued secondary text relies on `#64748B` (Slate Grey), and auxiliary tertiary captions lean on `#94A3B8`.
- **Canvas & Structural Ground:**
  - App Background: `#F7F8F6` (Calm, warm off-white parchment feel)
  - Surface Panels / Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp clinical white)
  - Structural Dividing Lines: `#E2E8F0` (Architectural hairline borders)
  - Alternate Row Stripes: `#F8FAFC`

Colors must strictly signify data status rather than purely cosmetic decoration.

## Typography

The typography couples the historical authority of central banking ledgers with the functional precision of database records.

- **Headlines & Display (`Newsreader`):** Employs medium-to-semibold weights with optical sizing. Gives balance statements, portfolio summaries, ledger titles, and analytical module headers their editorial weight.
- **Body, UI & Form Labels (`Inter`):** Handles operational UI mechanics, system instructions, filter chips, and forms. Optimized for neutral clarity and strict rendering fidelity across modern displays.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics (`Inter` / Monospace Fallback):** All financial figures, currency notations (specifically INR `₹`), transactional logs, timestamps, and relational database identifiers (`UUID`, `Rule_ID`) must enforce tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` or Tailwind's `tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment across stacked analytical records.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an enterprise-ready 12-column grid system tuned for dense analytical throughput. 

- **Structure:**
  - Desktop (≥1024px): 12-column configuration with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter and 32px (`2rem`) outer frame margins. Max-width containment standardizes at `1440px` for optimal tabular readability without extreme eye-scan drift.
  - Tablet (768px – 1023px): 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) page padding.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins. Tabular analytics automatically convert into segmented card stacks or horizontally scrollable frozen-column tables.
- **Density Philosophy:** Compact internal component spacing keeps interactive queries and key analytical views visible above the fold. Spacing between parent sections follows a disciplined `2.5rem` step, while component-level item packing stays tightly grouped between `0.25rem` and `0.75rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects dramatic atmospheric drop-shadows, diffused multi-layered colored glows, and blurred glass surfaces. Depth is established through structural definition:

- **Surface Layering:** Flat `#FFFFFF` panels sit directly on top of the `#F7F8F6` canvas. A hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) provides physical separation without optical blur.
- **Shadow Scale:**
  - Level 0 (Flat): Base card panels, table rows, and nested metrics rely entirely on borders (`border border-slate-200`).
  - Level 1 (`shadow-sm`): Transient elements (context dropdowns, autocomplete suggestion popovers, filter menus) use a subtle, grounded shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - Level 2 (Modals & Deep Drawers): Modal query builders and audit detail panes use `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with an explicit border.
- **Interaction Feedback:** Focus rings and active states use clean, solid lines (e.g., `outline: 2px solid #0B1F3A`, `outline-offset: 1px`) rather than expansive outer glows.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a disciplined **Soft (`1`)** shape vocabulary. Radii are kept crisp and restrained to support high data density and formal institutional tone:

- **Containers & Data Cards:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) maximum.
- **Inputs & Action Buttons:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- **Metadata Badges & System Tags:** `0.25rem` (4px) or rounded-sm. Pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly forbidden except for binary status toggle handles, preserving an analytical rather than playful tone.
- **Charts & Histograms:** Internal bar corner radii are strictly set to 0px or 2px top corners to preserve exact geometric representation against Chart.js Cartesian axes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`) background, white text, 4px border radius, subtle hover shift to `#142C4F`. Padding: `0.5rem 1rem` for medium, `0.375rem 0.75rem` for compact data-dense panels.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#1B2430`. Hover state triggers subtle background tint `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive / Flag:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FCA5A5`.

### Data Cards & Panels
- **Container Architecture:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, hairline border (`#E2E8F0`), micro-padding (`1rem` to `1.25rem`).
- **Header:** Newsreader serif section title paired with a right-aligned utility slot (e.g., query runtime badge, export action, or timestamp).

### Input Fields & Structured Query Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Flat `#FFFFFF` interior, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px roundedness. Focus state applies a crisp navy boundary ring (`#0B1F3A`) with zero blur.
- **Currency Entry:** Persistent inline `₹` prefix locked in tabular font weight `#64748B`.

### Badges & Categorization Tags
- **Categorization Origin (e.g., 'Rule #4', 'Heuristic AI', 'Manual Audit'):** Compact micro-badges (`text-[10px] font-semibold tracking-wider uppercase`).
  - Rule-based: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, `#E2E8F0` border.
  - Automated / Heuristic: `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#A7F3D0` border.
  - Review / Flagged: `#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, `#FDE68A` border.

### Tables & Tabular Ledgers
- **Layout:** Dense structure with explicit cell padding (`py-2.5 px-3.5`).
- **Header:** Sticky top position, `#F8FAFC` background, hairline border bottom (`#E2E8F0`), all caps 11px slate typography (`#64748B`).
- **Row Styling:** Hover highlight using `#F1F5F9`. Monetary values use right-aligned monospace / tabular-num format.

### Progress Bars & Budget Threshold Gauges
- **Track:** Hairline track height (6px to 8px), flat `#E2E8F0` track background, 2px corner radius.
- **Fill Behavior:** 
  - Standard / Healthy: Solid Money Green (`#1B9C6E`).
  - Approaching Limit (80–99%): Muted Warm Gold (`#D4A017`).
  - Breached (≥100%): Deep Amber/Crimson (`#DC2626`). Transitions animate linearly without bouncy spring curves.
